-------------------------

Step 3: Restart Your Computer
-----------------------------

Restarting Windows after uninstalling Vivaldi can help remove temporary files and refresh system settings.

Step 4: Download Vivaldi Again
------------------------------

Download the latest version of Vivaldi Browser installer.

Wait until the download completes before opening the file.

Step 5: Install Vivaldi
-----------------------

Open the installer and follow the setup instructions.

Choose your preferred installation settings and allow the process to complete.

Once installed, launch Vivaldi and configure your preferences.
